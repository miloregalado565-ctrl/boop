The 12-Hour Playbook

This is the part I can't automate for you: contacting real businesses and taking payment. Everything else — finding candidates, building the site, writing the outreach — is done in the generator tool. Follow this in order.

HOUR 0–1 · Get paid before you sell anything

Create a free Stripe account and make a Payment Link (Stripe Dashboard → Payment links → New) for $397 "Website Build" and one for $97/mo "Managed Website." No code needed. Keep both links handy — you'll drop them straight into replies. This is the single step that turns a "yes" into money in your account, so do it first.

HOUR 1–3 · Build a list of 20–30 qualified leads

Open Google Maps. Search auto repair near [your city] (or HVAC, plumbing, mobile detailing — auto repair has the highest no-website rate: ~45%). For each result:

  • Rating 4.5★ or higher, 30+ reviews (proof they're good and active)
  • Click the listing — no "Website" button, or a broken/Facebook-only link
  • Note: name, city, rating, review count, phone, address, top services, and copy 2–3 of their actual review text

Put this in a simple spreadsheet or notes doc. Aim for 20–30 rows — that volume is what makes the numbers work, since even a great cold outreach converts a small percentage.

HOUR 3–5 · Generate a preview + outreach for every lead

Open the generator tool. For each business: paste in its details, hit Generate. You instantly get (1) a live one-page site preview you can actually open and click through, (2) a downloadable HTML file, and (3) a personalized email, text, and phone script referencing their real rating and reviews. The lead is auto-saved to the tracker tab so nothing gets lost.

Do this for all 20–30 leads before you send a single message — batching this way is what makes it fast.

HOUR 5–8 · Send outreach — phone first, then text/email

Phone calls close same-day; email/text sit in an inbox. Priority order:

  • Call using the phone script — ask for the owner by name if you have it, otherwise "whoever handles the website"
  • Text immediately after a call (even a good one) with the preview link — most people say yes after they actually see it
  • Email anyone you can't reach by phone, attaching or linking the downloaded HTML preview

Mark each lead's status in the tracker tab as you go: Found → Contacted → Replied → Closed.

HOUR 8–10 · Handle replies and close

When someone says "send me the link" or "how much" — that's a warm lead. Send the preview + one of your two Stripe payment links directly. Most objections are price or trust; the phone script's objection lines cover both. Don't discount below $397/$97 on the first call — hold price, offer to walk them through the preview live on a call instead.

HOUR 10–12 · Deliver on any "yes"

Once paid: point their domain (or set up a subdomain/new one via Netlify/Vercel, both free for a single static page) to the downloaded HTML file, or host it for them if they bought Managed. Send them the live link. That's a closed sale, delivered, same day.

Reality check

This plan gets you from zero to "in front of paying customers" within 12 hours if you actually make the calls and sends — I can't do that outreach for you, and I won't fabricate a guaranteed sale. Cold outreach conversion is realistically single-digit to low-double-digit percent even done well, which is why the lead list needs to be 20–30 deep, not 3–5.
