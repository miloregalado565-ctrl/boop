FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to make an account?

No. Flashly runs entirely in your browser and saves your decks locally. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your decks live in your browser's local storage, so clearing site data or switching browsers/devices will reset them. Pro users can export decks to CSV as a backup.

What spaced-repetition method does it use?

A simplified version of the well-known SM-2 algorithm: cards you know well get spaced out further, cards you struggle with come back sooner.

Is this a subscription?

No. Pro is a single one-time payment for lifetime access to that browser/device.
